Session Playlist Builder

Builds session playlists in your Spotify account from your own playlists. Preview first, then create.

1. Connect your Spotify app

This tool works through a free Spotify developer app that belongs to you. Use an app made just for this tool. If you have a Spotify app from another project, leave it alone and create a new one. Pick the option that fits:

  1. Open the Spotify developer dashboard (it opens in a new tab), log in, and click Create app.
  2. Fill in the form:
    • App name: type Playlist Builder
    • App description: leave it blank. (If Spotify will not let you continue without one, type anything.)
    • Website: leave it blank, if you see this box.
    • Redirect URI: copy this address, paste it in exactly as shown, then click Add:
    • Which API/SDKs are you planning to use? Check Web API only. Leave the other boxes unchecked.
    • Check the box agreeing to Spotify's terms, then click Save.
  3. Open your new app's Settings and copy the Client ID.
  1. Open the Spotify developer dashboard (it opens in a new tab), log in, and click the app you created for this tool.
  2. Open its Settings and copy the Client ID.

This address is not registered with your app yet. In your app's Settings, paste it under Redirect URIs, click Add, then scroll down and click Save. Then try Connect again.

Your Client ID is not a secret. It stays in this browser, and nothing is sent anywhere except to Spotify.
