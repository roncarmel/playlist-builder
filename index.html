Session Playlist Builder

Builds session playlists in your Spotify account from your own playlists. Preview first, then create.

1. Connect your Spotify app

This tool works through a free Spotify developer app that belongs to you. Use an app made just for this tool. If you have a Spotify app from another project, leave it alone and create a new one. Pick the option that fits:

  1. Open the Spotify developer dashboard (it opens in a new tab), log in, and click Create app.
  2. Fill in the form:
    • App name: type Playlist Builder
    • App description: leave it blank. (If Spotify will not let you continue without one, type anything.)
    • Website: leave it blank, if you see this box.
    • Redirect URI: copy this address, paste it in exactly as shown, then click Add:
    • Which API/SDKs are you planning to use? Check Web API only. Leave the other boxes unchecked.
    • Check the box agreeing to Spotify's terms, then click Save.
  3. Open your new app's Settings and copy the Client ID.
  1. Open the Spotify developer dashboard (it opens in a new tab), log in, and click the app you created for this tool.
  2. Open its Settings and copy the Client ID.

2. Your formats

Edit your formats here. They are saved in this browser as you type.

Why back up? Your formats are saved in this browser as you type, but clearing your browser data erases them. A backup file keeps a copy on your computer. Restore it if your formats ever disappear, or to use them on another computer. It contains your formats only, not your Spotify sign-in.

3. Preview and create

Preview shows the playlists this would make, with songs picked at random. If it looks right, a Create button appears below the plan, and it creates exactly what you see.

Connect Spotify, then choose a format and click Preview plan.

      More options

      Limit how many playlists are made

      By default, Preview plans as many playlists as your songs allow. To cap it, enter a number, then preview again.

      playlists per format

      Remove earlier playlists

      Removes playlists from your Spotify library whose names match a format's naming pattern, for example "mix 1", "mix 2". It only touches playlists you own, and it asks before removing anything. It needs Spotify to be connected.

      Clear saved data

      If something looks stuck, you can make this page forget everything it saved in this browser: your Client ID, your formats, and your Spotify sign-in. Nothing in your Spotify account changes.
