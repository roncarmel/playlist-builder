More about this app

What it does
Ceremony Playlist Builder makes long playlists from your existing Spotify playlists. Describe the structure of the playlists you want to create, and the app builds as many complete playlists as your music allows, with songs picked at random and no song repeated across the playlists made from one format. Each playlist is created in your Spotify account, ready to play.

What it's good for
Ceremonies, retreats, workshops, and any long event where the music should follow an arc, such as arriving, deepening, and landing. Instead of steering a shuffle during the event or manually building a playlist ahead of time for each event, set the structure once and get multiple fresh, non-overlapping playlists. You can then use these playlists for subsequent events without ever repeating the same music.

How it works

  1. Connect Spotify through a free developer app. This takes a few minutes to set up, but you’ll only need to do it once.
  2. Define your format(s): which of your playlists to draw from, in what order, and for how long.
  3. Create the playlists. The app shows you what it found and asks before it makes anything.

What you need
A Spotify Premium account (Spotify requires it for the free developer app) and some playlists of your own to draw from.

Reporting problems
If you run into a problem you can report it in the More options section at the bottom of this page.

Privacy
Your existing Spotify playlists are only read, never changed. Everything runs in this browser, and nothing about your music, such as playlist names or songs, goes anywhere except to you and Spotify. For the full details, see Privacy at the bottom of this page.

2. Edit your formats

Your formats are saved in this browser as you type. You can any time.

How formats work

A format is a recipe for one kind of playlist: which of your playlists to draw from, in what order, and for how long. The app makes as many playlists from a format as your songs allow, and no song repeats between them.

Segments

Each row is one stretch of music. Tap the playlist to choose it, and use the minus and plus buttons to set how many minutes it fills. Drag the dotted handle to change the order. Each segment typically lands within about 30 seconds of its target. Point at a row, or tap a piece of the bar at the top, to see which belongs to which. Add another with New segment.

Silence

The small tags hanging off the right side of the rows are silences. A silence adds time on top of your segment minutes, and can be used for effect or simply to tell a facilitator the music has moved into a new segment. To put the same silence between every two segments, open the format's three dots and set Silence between segments. Tap a tag to change that one silence. A tag with its own length is a deeper color, and Reset returns it to the default. To add a silence where there is none, use a segment's three dots.

Mixes

To blend two playlists in one segment, tap the three dots on that row and choose Add a mix. Pick the second playlist, then use the slider to set how much of each. In the bar at the top, a mixed segment shows as diagonal stripes in two shades of the accent color.

Names

The name of a format is also the name of the playlists it makes: a format called "Warm focus" makes "Warm focus 1", "Warm focus 2", and so on. Give every format a different name. When you create playlists again, this app treats playlists that match a format's name as its own and offers to replace them.

      Privacy

      
        What stays in this browser
        
      
        Your Client ID, your formats, your color choice, and a copy of the songs from your playlists are saved in this browser, and nowhere else. The copy of your songs lets the app skip asking Spotify for them again. Disconnect removes your Spotify sign-in and the saved songs. Clear saved data, in More options, removes everything.
        

      
        Your Spotify account
        
      
        You sign in on Spotify's own page, and this app never sees your Spotify password. It asks Spotify for permission to read your playlists, create playlists, remove playlists, and set their cover images. Your existing playlists are only read, never changed. Removing only touches the playlists this app made, unless you choose to remove others too.
        
      
        Playlists created by this app stay off your Spotify profile and out of search, though anyone with a playlist's link can still open it. To change that, use Make private in the Spotify app.
        

      
        Who the page talks to
        
      
        Spotify, for everything about your music. The page's code comes from GitHub, and the Python it runs comes from a public code host (jsDelivr). Ko-fi's page loads only if you open Donate, and the report form opens only if you choose to report a problem.
        

      
        Anonymous visit counts
        
      
        The page counts visits with GoatCounter, a service that uses no cookies and does not use unique identifiers to follow people around the web. It records that the page was opened, with the browser, screen size, country, and the site you came from, but not who you are. It also counts a few actions: the first time a browser connects to Spotify, how many playlists get created, when Donate is opened, and the kind of connection problem that happened. It never receives playlist names, formats, song titles, or your Client ID. A small marker in this browser remembers that your first connection was counted, so it is counted once.
        

      
        Donations
        
      
        Donations are handled entirely by Ko-fi and its payment providers. This app never sees any payment details.
        

      
        Problem reports
        
      
        A problem report is sent only if you choose to send it, and it goes only to me, the developer of this app. The form opens in a new tab, and nothing is sent until you submit it there. The details attached are shown in More options first: the app version, browser, window size, last error, and recent steps. Your sign-in, Client ID, playlist names, and song titles are never included.
        

      
        Accounts and ads
        
      
        There are no accounts and no ads.
