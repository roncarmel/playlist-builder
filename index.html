1. Connect your Spotify app

This app works through a free Spotify developer app that belongs to you. Set it up once:

  1. Open your Spotify developer dashboard (it opens in a new tab) and log in. Click your app. If you do not have one yet, click Create app, name it anything, and choose Web API.
  2. Click Settings, then find the Redirect URIs box.
  3. Copy this address and paste it into that box, exactly as shown:
    Click Add, then scroll down and click Save.
  4. On the same Settings page, copy your app's Client ID and paste it here:
  5. Click Connect Spotify. Spotify will ask you to approve read-only access, then bring you back to this page.

Your Client ID is not a secret. It stays in this browser, and nothing is sent anywhere except to Spotify.
